# Current results

[`current-results.json`](current-results.json) separates three evidence populations:

1. the complete 105-sample controlled corpus Judge validation;
2. the three-problem live natural-output MVP, of which two samples completed process review;
3. the later 75-sample balanced pilot, which failed closed after one completed result.

Do not combine their numerators or denominators. In particular, 105/105 is a corpus/Judge
acceptance result, not model accuracy, while 2/2 is too small and completion-selected to be a
formal estimate.
